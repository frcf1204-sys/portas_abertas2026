Influence of Games

Games have become an easily accessible pastime thanks to the internet and the evolution of technology, bringing both benefits and points of attention for the youth.

🧠 Cognitive and Social Benefits ▼

Games require strategy, quick decision making, and hand-eye coordination. A scientific research from 2026, gathering 133 studies and 14,245 participants, found significant associations between playing video games and the improvement of skills such as:

  • Memory: Need to memorize maps, patterns, and rules.
  • Visual Attention: Tracking multiple elements on the screen simultaneously.
  • Intelligence and Cognitive Control: Planning and adapting to new strategies.

Furthermore, multiplayer games help in socialization, cooperation, and teamwork.

📚 Impact on Education and Learning ▼

Essays shows that digital games present better learning outcomes. They encourage students to analyze information, test possibilities, and solve problems.

An analysis with 3,894 students demonstrated a moderate positive effect on STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) education. In addition, constantly exposing players to dialogues and instructions makes games great allies in teaching a second language.

⚠️ Health Risks and Gaming Disorder ▼

Despite the benefits, excessive use presents risks. The WHO recognizes Gaming Disorder (ICD-11) as a behavior pattern where there is difficulty in controlling gaming, prioritizing it over other activities, causing significant impairment for at least 12 months.

  • Hearing: Sound levels in headphones can reach 91 dB. The WHO recommends keeping the volume below 60% and using speakers to avoid hearing loss and tinnitus.
  • Physical Health: Long sessions lead to a sedentary lifestyle, obesity, musculoskeletal problems, and severe sleep deprivation.
  • Mental Health: A 2024 review with 3,683 adolescents linked problematic gaming to anxiety, depression, and emotional problems.

Games and Cognitive Stimulation

Certain game categories go beyond entertainment, acting as true "brain exercises". They help delay symptoms of neurodegenerative diseases and assist neurodivergent individuals in focusing and improving logical connections.

1. Tetris

Studies indicate that fast spatial processing games, like Tetris, help develop spatial skills and visual focus. It is widely used in research regarding trauma and cognitive capacity.

2. Brain Age (Dr. Kawashima)

Developed specifically to train the brain, this title uses math, reading, and memorization challenges to stimulate the prefrontal cortex, highly recommended for delaying cognitive decline in the elderly.

3. Portal 2

A first-person puzzle game that requires lateral thinking, complex problem-solving, and three-dimensional perception. It is highly effective for cognitive flexibility and logical reasoning.
